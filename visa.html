💰 1. Blocked Account (Sperrkonto)
Requirements for depositing the mandatory €11,904 annual financial proof (€992/month) via certified providers.
🏥 2. Health Insurance (Krankenversicherung)
Statutory public insurance (TK/AOK/Barmer) at student rates (~€120–€130/mo) vs. private travel insurance coverage.
📄 3. Embassy Visa Checklist
Complete document assembly, digital application submission, consular fee (€75), and embassy interview strategies.
🏘️ 4. On-Arrival Execution
City registration (Anmeldung within 14 days), opening a local German bank account, and residence permit booking.
