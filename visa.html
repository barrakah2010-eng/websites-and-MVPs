Student Visa Relocation Checklist

Essential prerequisites for your German national visa application.

1

University Admission Letter

Official letter of acceptance from a recognized public institution.

2

Blocked Account (Sperrkonto)

Proof of financial resources deposited into a regulated provider (e.g., Fintiba / Expatrio).

3

Health Insurance Coverage

Travel health insurance leading into statutory public health coverage (TK or AOK).
