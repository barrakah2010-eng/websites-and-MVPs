1. Accommodation & Housing (Wohnungssuche)
How to secure student housing (WG), get your Wohnungsgeberbestätigung, and complete City Registration (Anmeldung).
+
Step-by-Step Housing Strategy: 1. Student dormitories (Studierendenwerk): Apply the exact day you submit your university application. Spaces are assigned on a strict waitlist basis. 2. Shared Apartments (Wohngemeinschaft / WG): Use WG-Gesucht.de. Craft a personalized profile in German outlining your lifestyle, clean habits, and funding. 3. Temporary Stay: Book a temporary stay (Zwischenmiete or hostel) for your first 2-3 weeks to attend flat viewings in person. Crucial Legal Document — Wohnungsgeberbestätigung: In Germany, you cannot register your residence without a landlord confirmation certificate (Wohnungsgeberbestätigung). Ensure your landlord explicitly permits city registration (Anmeldung). Without it, you cannot obtain a tax ID or open a traditional bank account.
2. Blocked Account Setup (Sperrkonto)
Official BaFin-compliant financial proof required for the German National Student Visa (Visum zu Studienzwecken).
+
Required Funding & Setup Workflow: 1. Required Amount: As mandated by German embassy regulations, students must deposit sufficient funds into a blocked account before their embassy appointment. 2. Approved Digital Providers: Use established digital platforms such as Fintiba or Expatrio for online verification in under 24 hours. 3. Monthly Payouts: Once you arrive in Germany and complete city registration, funds are released in monthly installments directly into your checking account (Girokonto).
